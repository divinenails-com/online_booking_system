About us

Our story

Founded in 2026 by Lehlogonolo Makofane, Divine Nails was born from a passion for beauty, precision, and self-care. After 3 years of experience in the nail industry, we envisioned a salon where every client receives not just a service, but a moment of genuine relaxation and personal attention.

From the moment you walk through our doors, you can expect a warm welcome, a clean and serene environment, and a team dedicated to understanding your individual style. Whether you are visiting for a classic manicure or intricate nail art, we take the time to ensure every detail is perfect — because your hands deserve nothing less.

Our mission is to provide exceptional nail care in an environment that prioritizes hygiene, artistry, and client comfort above all else. We believe that beautiful nails are a reflection of confidence, and we are committed to helping every client leave feeling polished, refreshed, and truly cared for.

Privacy Policy

Divine Nails ("we", "the salon") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa.

The responsible party is Divine Nails, Mankweng Stand 401, Unit E, Polokwane 0727. Contact: Mr.Sekgobelaa@gmail.com · +27 71 064 8238.

2. What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address — provided through the booking form;
  • Booking details: service, date, time, stylist preference and notes;
  • Correspondence you send us via WhatsApp, phone or email.

3. Why we collect it

  • To schedule, confirm and manage your appointment;
  • To contact you about your booking (including cancellations or load-shedding-related changes);
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Booking confirmations are sent via WhatsApp (Meta Platforms). This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

5. Storage & security

Booking records are kept on access-controlled systems, visible only to authorised salon staff, and retained only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

6. Your rights

Under POPIA you may request access to your personal information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its processing. Email Mr.Sekgobelaa@gmail.com — we respond within a reasonable time, at no charge.

7. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): complaints.IR@inforegulator.org.za · www.inforegulator.org.za.
